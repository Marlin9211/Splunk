Step 2 · Deployment Trade-off Board

Interrogate each option

Open each deployment model. Decide whether it is a strong fit, possible but compromised, or a weak fit for Harborline. Your notes will carry into the final decision.

Standalone

One Splunk instance performs ingestion, indexing, search, and visualization.

Distributed

Multiple Splunk instances perform one or more specialized functions.

Cloud

Similar to distributed, but hosted and managed by a third-party cloud provider.

Learning Transfer

Carry the mental model into Splunk.

When you next open Splunk, connect interface actions to the system behind them: machine data originates somewhere, forwarders can collect it, indexers make it searchable, and the search experience helps you investigate and turn data into operational insight.

Navigate

Recognize the web-based interface, search bar, results, navigation, dashboards, and visualization editors. Splunk browser access uses port 8000 in the lesson.

Configure thoughtfully

Account and preference settings matter. In particular, aligning the time zone helps timestamps make sense during analysis.

Extend when needed

Splunk apps package dashboards, reports, and other content; add-ons provide additional functionality such as inputs and parsers for specific data sources.
